Field Ready Admin Website Guide

The Field Ready Admin website is where staff manage what the app works from: customers and machines, people and stores, inspection forms, subscriptions, and machine options. Open it at akendall72-tech.github.io/FieldReady and sign in with your Field Ready email and password. The app has its own guide, the Field Ready Field Guide.

At a glance

The website saves straight to the server, and every phone syncs with it on its own schedule. Each person, and each phone, only ever gets what their role and store allow.

Signing in and what each role can open

Sign in with the same email and password you use in the Field Ready app. Each role sees only the pages it needs, listed along the top.

Customer logins can't use the website; it tells them to use the app instead. If you see No website access, your login doesn't have a staff role. Forgot your password? Ask an owner or admin for a new temporary one.

The first time you sign in with a temporary password, the website asks you to Choose a password of your own (at least 8 characters) before anything else. Use that password in the app too.

Customers & machines

This page keeps the same customer and machine records the app uses, so office staff can set up equipment before a technician arrives. Changes here reach phones at their next sync; inspections themselves are done only in the app.

Finding a customer

Use Search for a customer, machine, or serial number, and the Store filter to narrow to one store (owners see Every store). Turn on Show archived to include archived customers and machines. Each customer is listed with their machines underneath; a machine tagged Changed by the customer was added or edited in the customer's own app.

Adding and editing a customer

  1. Tap Add Customer, or Edit on a customer.
  2. Enter the Name, and the Phone, Email, and home Store.
  3. Tap Save.

Archive hides a customer and their machines from lists in the app and here; their history stays and you can bring them back. Delete Customer removes them with their machines everywhere. A customer with any finalized inspection can be archived but not deleted. Only owners, and admins at the customer's store, can archive or delete.

A machine's page

Tap a machine, or Add Machine under a customer, to open its page. It has the same parts as the app's machine screens:

  • Details: customer, equipment type, manufacturer, model, model year, serial number, where it's kept, and the store.
  • Technology: guidance system, guidance software (only the chosen system's products), steering, and correction. Choose Unknown rather than guessing.
  • About this machine: the equipment type's questions. Leave any you can't confirm as Not answered; every check stays in.
  • Components: + Add component for each part, with its type, model, serial number, software or firmware, correction level for receivers, how sure you are, and notes.
  • Correction subscriptions: + Add subscription, choosing a listed service or Custom service, the account reference, expiration, source, and the date it was checked. Never enter passwords or login details.
  • Archive or delete: archive hides the machine and keeps its history; Delete Machine removes it with its technology, subscriptions, and draft inspections everywhere, and isn't offered once it has a finalized inspection.

Tap Save to keep your changes. If the customer changed the machine in their app, the page says so: check it, then Mark Checked, or save a change, to clear the tag. A machine at another store opens read-only.

Customer app logins

A customer can have their own logins to see their equipment, reports, and subscriptions in the app, and to add and update their machines.

  1. Under the customer, in App logins, tap + Add Login.
  2. Enter the Name of the person signing in and Their email, then Add Login.
  3. A temporary password appears once. Tap Copy and give it to them with these steps: install Field Ready, sign in with that email and password, then choose their own password. It isn't shown again after Done.

Each login can be turned off with Access off (they can't see anything until it's back on) or given a new temporary password with Reset Password. Owners, and admins at the customer's store, manage customer logins.

People & stores

This page decides who can sign in, with what role, and at which store. Owners manage everyone and the stores; admins add and manage the employees at their own store.

RoleCan do
OwnerEverything, at every store
AdminTheir store, its people, and How to Check steps on forms
EmployeeInspections at their store

Adding a person

  1. Tap Add Person.
  2. Enter their name and email, choose a Role, and choose their Store (owners can have Every store).
  3. Save. Their login is created with a temporary password, shown once.
  4. Tap Copy and give it to them. They sign in to the Field Ready app with their email and that password, then choose their own. It isn't shown again after Done.

Managing people

Use Show to list everyone or one store. Tap Edit on a person to change their name, role, or store, or to turn off Can sign in to Field Ready. Turning access off stops them signing in and syncing; their inspections stay. Reset Password makes a new temporary password and stops the old one working.

You can't turn off your own access, and the only owner always stays an owner with access: make someone else an owner first. Waiting for a login lists invitations that never became logins; cancel them and add the person again.

Stores

Add a store by name. Turning a store's In use off removes it from the app's store pickers; its machines, people, and history stay.

Contacts for customers

Each store's contacts are the people its customers see under Who to contact on their app's Home screen. Add a name, phone, and email for each role: PTx Product Specialist, Sales Representative, Customer Service Representative, and Service. Owners edit every store's contacts; admins edit their own store's.

Account deletion requests

When someone taps Delete Account in the app, owners see them here under Asked to delete their login. Delete Login removes the login for good; inspections they did and their machines stay as service records. Decline keeps the login; let them know why. Apple requires that people can ask for this, so answer requests promptly.

Inspection forms

This page holds the forms the app inspects from. Phones always use each form's newest published version. Your changes save on their own as a draft and reach phones only when they're published.

Guidance forms

The app gives each machine the Guidance form for the guidance system set in its technology:

  • Factory Guidance for every factory-integrated system, such as Fendt Guide and MF Guide.
  • PTx, John Deere, Raven, and Ag Leader each have their own form.
  • Other for any other system, and for machines whose system isn't known.

A factory-integrated guidance system added on Machine options uses Factory Guidance. Any other new system shows in the dropdown as "uses Other"; an owner can choose it and tap Create a Form for it, which starts as a copy of the Other form.

Machine forms

Each machine type has one form, picked from the Machine type dropdown. A type marked "no form yet" has no Machine inspection: an owner chooses Start from (a blank form, or a copy of another type's form) and taps Create Form. An older checklist that covers several types shows a note naming the other types it's used for.

Editing a form

  1. Pick the tab, then the system or type. The form opens ready to edit.
  2. Make your changes. They save on their own as a draft a moment after you stop typing; the line under the title says when. Phones keep the published version meanwhile.
  3. Fix anything listed under Fix before publishing.
  4. Tap Publish to Phones (owners; for Custom, the maker). Phones pick it up at their next sync. Discard Changes throws the draft away instead.

Admins can change How to Check steps; an owner publishes them.

Custom checklists

New Checklist starts one: give it a name, choose Start from (blank, or a copy of a form you can see), and tick the equipment types it's for (none: any machine). Only you see it and use it in the app. Retire stops your phone offering it; Show retired lists retired ones, and Bring Back returns one.

What publishing does to inspections

Draft inspections move to the new version the next time they're opened. New checks appear, wording updates, and removed checks go, including any answer recorded for them. Finalized inspections always keep the version they were signed with. A draft inspection keeps its form even if the machine's guidance system changes.

Subscriptions

This page holds the correction services technicians pick from when they record a machine's subscription, grouped by provider (such as Trimble, John Deere, Raven, and Ag Leader). Owners change it; admins can look. Each change saves as soon as you leave the field or tick the box, and phones pick it up at their next sync.

  • Name and Note for each service. The name is what's saved on subscriptions.
  • Offered in the app: untick it to stop offering a service. Subscriptions that already use it keep it.
  • + Add service under a provider: its name, correction type, and an optional note.
  • Add a provider at the bottom of the page, then add its first service.

Technicians can still type in a service that isn't listed.

Machine options

Machine options are the choices technicians pick from when they describe a machine, and some of their settings decide which checks an inspection includes. Only owners can change them; admins can look.

Working with a list

  1. Choose a list on the left.
  2. Rename an option by editing its Name. What the app stores never changes, so renaming is always safe.
  3. Turn Offered in the app off to hide an option from new choices. Records that already use it keep it and still show it. A few options the app relies on (such as Unknown and Other) can't be hidden.
  4. Use the arrows to change the order the app lists them in.
  5. Add an option with a name and Add. A new option can be removed until it's saved; after that, hide it instead.
  6. Fix anything under Fix before saving, then Save Changes (or Discard Changes).

For lists with Used on settings, Show options used on filters the list to one equipment type.

How changes reach the phones

The website and the app share one set of records on the server. A change you save here reaches each phone at its next sync, which runs every 5 minutes while the app is open, when it's opened, a few seconds after the technician saves something, and from Sync Now in the app's Settings.

You change on the websiteWhat phones do
A customer, machine, technology, or subscriptionShow it after their next sync
A machine option or its settingsOffer the new choices after their next sync
A published formUse it for new inspections; draft inspections move to it when they're next opened
A retired Custom checklistStop offering it for new inspections; inspections already using it keep it
A store's contactsUpdate the customers' Who to contact
A person's access turned offThey can't sign in or sync until it's back on

It works the other way too. Customers and machines added or changed in the app appear here after that phone syncs, and each machine shows how many finalized inspections it has. The inspections themselves, with their photos and reports, are read in the app. A phone without a signal keeps everything safe and syncs when it's back online. A machine a customer changes in their app shows Changed by the customer here and in the app until someone checks it.

Setting up a new organization

This part is for Prime Theory Labs. Platform admins see an Organizations page that dealers' staff don't. It lists every organization using Field Ready with its stores, how many staff and customer logins it has, and its owners. It shows names and counts only; you can't open or change another organization's records from it.

To add a dealer:

  1. Tap New Organization.
  2. Enter the dealer's name, its first store, and the owner's name and email. The email can't already have a Field Ready login: each login belongs to one organization.
  3. Tap Create Organization. It makes the organization, the store, and the owner's login, and loads the default option lists, the Guidance forms, and the Precision Planting and starter Machine forms.
  4. A temporary password and a message for the owner appear once. Tap Copy Message (or Copy for the password alone), send it to them, then tap Done.

The owner signs in, chooses their own password, and sets up the rest on the pages above: stores, people, customers, options, and forms. Until they choose a password, their line says Hasn't chosen a password yet. If they lose the temporary password, New Temporary Password next to their name makes another.
