Field Ready

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Who we are

Field Ready is made by Prime Theory Labs ("we"). Farm equipment dealers use it with their staff and their customers to inspect equipment and its precision technology. Each dealer creates the accounts for its own people and customers; you can't sign up on your own. The dealer that gave you your account decides what its records hold and who at the dealer can see them. We run the app and its servers for the dealer.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address, and role, the dealer and store you work with, and, for customer accounts, which customer (farm) you belong to.
  • Customer and equipment records: customer names, phone numbers, and email addresses; machines and their details, technology, and correction subscriptions. Subscription records hold reference numbers only, never passwords.
  • Inspection records: inspection results and notes, photos taken in the app, signatures, and the PDF reports made from them. Location information is removed from photos before they're saved.

We don't collect your location, contacts, browsing, or advertising identifiers. The app has no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites. We don't sell information.

How it's used

Only to run Field Ready: to record and report inspections, to keep records in step between devices, and to let customers see their own equipment, subscriptions, and finished inspection reports.

Who can see it

  • Your dealer's staff, according to their role and store.
  • Customers see only their own farm's equipment, subscriptions, and finished inspections, and their store's contacts.
  • Other dealers never see your dealer's records.
  • Our service providers: Supabase stores the records, photos, and reports and handles sign-in for us. Apple distributes the app. They handle information only to provide those services.

We share information otherwise only if the law requires it.

How it's kept

Records are saved on your device and on Supabase's servers. Information travels encrypted, and access rules on the server limit each person to what their role allows. Inspection records are kept as the dealer's service records, including after the person who made them leaves.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, open Settings › Delete Account. Your dealer then deletes your login. Inspections and equipment records stay with the dealer as service records.
  • See or correct your information: ask your dealer, or contact us (below).

Children

Field Ready is for equipment dealers' staff and customers and isn't directed to children under 13.
