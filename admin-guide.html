Field Ready Admin Website Guide

The Field Ready Admin website is where staff manage what the app works from: customers and machines, people and stores, inspection forms, subscriptions, and machine options. Its Dashboard shows how inspections and subscriptions are going. Open it at fieldready.agri-barn.com (the old github.io address takes you there) and sign in with your Field Ready email and password. The app has its own guide, the Field Ready Field Guide.

At a glance

The website saves straight to the server, and every phone syncs with it on its own schedule. Each person, and each phone, only ever gets what their role and store allow.

An owner can tick Can edit forms and options for an admin or employee who knows the equipment well (see Managing people). Until an owner unticks it, they edit and publish every inspection form and change Subscriptions and Machine options, as owners do; employees with it see those two pages too.

Owners and admins open on the Dashboard; employees on Customers & machines. Customer logins see only Dashboard (their own farm), My equipment (their farm's machines, to add and keep up to date), and Account; see Customers on the website. If you see No website access, your login has no access to Field Ready. Forgot your password? Ask an owner or admin to email you a password link.

New logins get an email with a link to choose a password; once chosen, it works on the website and in the app. If you've turned on two-step sign-in (see Account), the website then asks for the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. If you sign in with a temporary password instead, the website first asks you to Choose a password of your own (at least 10 characters).

Dashboard

Owners and admins open the website on the Dashboard: how inspections and subscriptions are going, at a glance. Admins see their store; owners see every store, or pick one with Store. An inspection appears once it's finalized and the phone that did it has synced. Refresh reads everything again, and the page refreshes on its own when you come back to it after a few minutes.

  • The tiles: Inspections finished in the last 30 days (and how many of them customers did); Not ready, the machines whose newest inspection failed a check; Due for inspection, the active machines not inspected in the last 12 months; and Subscriptions to renew, expired or due within 90 days.
  • Needs follow-up (only when there's one): each machine whose newest inspection failed a check.
  • Recent inspections (your technicians') and Customer inspections (done by customers in the app), newest first, with Show more for older ones. Each shows its result the way the report works it out (Ready, Ready With Recommendations, Not Ready, or Incomplete) and what it found, such as "1 failed check · 1 recommendation". Click one to see the failed checks and recommendations with their notes, who did it, and Download Report for the PDF the app made.
  • Subscription tracker: the bar splits the correction subscriptions on active machines into Expired, Within 30 days, 31–90 days, Current, and No date, with the count of each. To renew lists the expired and due ones, soonest first; All lists every one. A subscription tied to a receiver shows it ("Receiver AG-25 · S/N 1234 · High").

Archived machines and customers are left out of the tiles and the tracker; their inspections stay in the lists.

Customers & machines

This page keeps the same customer and machine records the app uses, so office staff can set up equipment before a technician arrives. Changes here reach phones at their next sync; inspections themselves are done only in the app.

Finding a customer

Use Search for a customer, machine, or serial number, and the Store filter to narrow to one store (owners see Every store). Turn on Show archived to include archived customers and machines. Each customer is listed with their machines underneath; a machine tagged Changed by the customer was added or edited in the customer's own app.

Adding and editing a customer

  1. Tap Add Customer, or Edit on a customer.
  2. Enter the Name, and the Phone, Email, and home Store. Email this farm your offers is ticked for new customers; it's unticked when the farm uses the stop link in an offer email, so tick it again only if they ask.
  3. Tap Save.

Archive hides a customer and their machines from lists in the app and here; their history stays and you can bring them back. Delete Customer removes them with their machines everywhere. A customer with any finalized inspection can be archived but not deleted. Only owners, and admins at the customer's store, can archive or delete.

A machine's page

Tap a machine, or Add Machine under a customer, to open its page. It has the same parts as the app's machine screens:

  • Details: customer, equipment type, manufacturer, model, model year, serial number, where it's kept, and the store.
  • Technology: guidance system, guidance software (only the chosen system's products), steering, and correction. Choose Unknown rather than guessing.
  • About this machine: the equipment type's questions. Leave any you can't confirm as Not answered; every check stays in.
  • Components: + Add component for each part, with its type, model, serial number, software or firmware, correction level for receivers, how sure you are, and notes. On aftermarket guidance (PTx, Raven, and other systems that aren't factory-integrated), a GNSS receiver says how many subscriptions belong to it and has Move to: choose another of the customer's machines that can have guidance, then Move. The receiver goes with its details, correction level, and subscriptions; each machine's correction source stays as it is. Moves go in the owners' Activity page. Removing a receiver keeps its subscriptions on the machine, untied.
  • Correction subscriptions: + Add subscription, choosing a listed service or Custom service, the account reference, expiration, source, and the date it was checked. Never enter passwords or login details. On aftermarket guidance choose the Receiver it belongs to first (add the receiver under Components if it isn't there); it can't be saved without one. On factory guidance (Fendt Guide, MF Guide) it goes with the machine.
  • Archive or delete: archive hides the machine and keeps its history; Delete Machine removes it with its technology, subscriptions, and draft inspections everywhere, and isn't offered once it has a finalized inspection.

Tap Save to keep your changes. If the customer changed the machine in their app, the page says so: check it, then Mark Checked, or save a change, to clear the tag. A machine at another store opens read-only.

Customer app logins

A customer can have their own logins to see their equipment, reports, and subscriptions in the app, and to add and update their machines.

  1. Under the customer, in App logins, tap + Add Login.
  2. Enter the Name of the person signing in and Their email, then tap Send Invite.
  3. Field Ready emails them a link to choose their password. On an iPhone or iPad it opens the Field Ready app; on a computer they can choose it on the page. The link works once.

Each login can be turned off with Access off (they can't see anything until it's back on) or sent a new link with Send Password Link (their current password keeps working until they choose a new one). Owners, and admins at the customer's store, manage customer logins. If an email can't be sent, see Adding a person.

People & stores

This page decides who can sign in, with what role, and at which store. Owners manage everyone and the stores; admins add and manage the employees at their own store.

Adding a person

  1. Tap Add Person.
  2. Enter their name and email, choose a Role, and choose their Store (owners can have Every store).
  3. Tap Send Invite. Their login is created, and Field Ready emails them a link to choose their password (from fieldready@agri-barn.com; replies come to you).
  4. A card confirms the email went. If it couldn't be sent, the card says why and shows the link: tap Copy Link and send it to them yourself (email or text). It isn't shown again after Done.

On an iPhone or iPad with the app, the link opens the Field Ready app, which asks them to choose a password (at least 10 characters); anywhere else they choose it on a web page, then sign in. The link works once, for 4 hours.

If the email already has a login with another organization, you'll see That email can't be added: use a different email for them. Each organization can send 50 sign-in emails (invitations and password links) a day.

Managing people

Use Show to list everyone or one store. Tap Edit on a person to change their name, role, or store, or to turn off Can sign in to Field Ready. Turning access off stops them signing in and syncing, and the app removes their records from their phone; their inspections stay on the server. Owners can also Reset Two-Step for someone who lost their authenticator (see Account), tick Can manage promotions for a staff member who should write offers (see Promotions; their row then shows a Promotions tag), tick Can edit forms and options for an admin or employee who should set up the inspection forms, Subscriptions, and Machine options for every store (their row shows a Forms & options tag; untick it when they're done), and tick Customer lead manager for whoever follows up with interested customers at their store (their row shows a Lead manager tag; see Promotions). Send Password Link emails them a link to choose a new password; their current password keeps working until they do.

You can't turn off your own access, and the only owner always stays an owner with access: make someone else an owner first. Waiting for a login lists invitations that never became logins; cancel them and add the person again.

Stores

Add a store by name. Turning a store's In use off removes it from the app's store pickers; its machines, people, and history stay.

Contacts for customers

Each store's contacts are the people its customers see under Who to contact on their app's Home screen. Add a name, phone, and email for each role: PTx Product Specialist, Sales Representative, Customer Service Representative, and Service. Owners edit every store's contacts; admins edit their own store's.

Account deletion requests

When someone taps Delete Account in the app, owners see them here under Asked to delete their login. Delete Login removes the login for good; inspections they did and their machines stay as service records. Decline keeps the login; let them know why. Apple requires that people can ask for this, so answer requests promptly.

Inspection forms

This page holds the forms the app inspects from. Phones always use each form's newest published version. Your changes save on their own as a draft and reach phones only when they're published.

Owners also see Customers can do inspections in the app at the top. While it's ticked, customer logins can inspect the machines on their own farm with your published forms (never anyone's Custom checklist). A customer's draft is theirs alone; you see the inspection once they finalize it, marked Done by the customer in the app and on its report. Untick it to stop new ones; inspections a customer already started can still be finished.

Guidance forms

The app gives each machine the Guidance form for the guidance system set in its technology:

  • Factory Guidance for every factory-integrated system, such as Fendt Guide and MF Guide.
  • PTx, John Deere, Raven, and Ag Leader each have their own form.
  • Other for any other system, and for machines whose system isn't known.

A factory-integrated guidance system added on Machine options uses Factory Guidance. Any other new system shows in the dropdown as "uses Other"; an owner (or someone allowed to edit forms and options) can choose it and tap Create a Form for it, which starts as a copy of the Other form.

Machine forms

Each machine type has one form, picked from the Machine type dropdown. A type marked "no form yet" has no Machine inspection: an owner (or someone allowed to edit forms and options) chooses Start from (a blank form, or a copy of another type's form) and taps Create Form. An older checklist that covers several types shows a note naming the other types it's used for.

Editing a form

  1. Pick the tab, then the system or type. The form opens ready to edit.
  2. Make your changes. They save on their own as a draft a moment after you stop typing; the line under the title says when. Phones keep the published version meanwhile.
  3. Fix anything listed under Fix before publishing.
  4. Tap Publish to Phones (owners and people an owner lets edit forms and options; for Custom, the maker). Phones pick it up at their next sync. Discard Changes throws the draft away instead.

Reviewing a form

Inspections in the app and their reports say whether a form has been reviewed, naming who reviewed it and when. The line under a form's title shows Not reviewed or the review. Mark a form once someone qualified has gone through every check and its How to Check steps against the manufacturer's procedures:

  1. Open the form and tap Mark as Reviewed (owners and people an owner lets edit forms and options).
  2. Enter who reviewed it (it starts with your name) and tap Mark as Reviewed. This saves as a change like any other.
  3. Tap Publish to Phones. Inspections with this version then show Form reviewed by that person, and their reports print it.

Any other change to the form takes the mark off, so mark it again once the changes are reviewed. A new form, or one made as a copy, starts unreviewed. Custom checklists have no review mark.

Custom checklists

New Checklist starts one: give it a name, choose Start from (blank, or a copy of a form you can see), and tick the equipment types it's for (none: any machine). Only you see it and use it in the app. Retire stops your phone offering it; Show retired lists retired ones, and Bring Back returns one.

What publishing does to inspections

Draft inspections move to the new version the next time they're opened. New checks appear, wording updates, and removed checks go, including any answer recorded for them. Finalized inspections always keep the version they were signed with. A draft inspection keeps its form even if the machine's guidance system changes.

Subscriptions

This page holds the correction services technicians pick from when they record a machine's subscription, grouped by provider (such as Trimble, John Deere, Raven, and Ag Leader). Owners, and people an owner lets edit forms and options, change it; other admins can look. Each change saves as soon as you leave the field or tick the box, and phones pick it up at their next sync.

  • Name and Note for each service. The name is what's saved on subscriptions.
  • Offered in the app: untick it to stop offering a service. Subscriptions that already use it keep it.
  • + Add service under a provider: its name, correction type, and an optional note.
  • Add a provider at the bottom of the page, then add its first service.

Technicians can still type in a service that isn't listed.

Machine options

Machine options are the choices technicians pick from when they describe a machine, and some of their settings decide which checks an inspection includes. Owners, and people an owner lets edit forms and options, change them; other admins can look.

Working with a list

  1. Choose a list on the left.
  2. Rename an option by editing its Name. What the app stores never changes, so renaming is always safe.
  3. Turn Offered in the app off to hide an option from new choices. Records that already use it keep it and still show it. A few options the app relies on (such as Unknown and Other) can't be hidden.
  4. Use the arrows to change the order the app lists them in.
  5. Add an option with a name and Add. A new option can be removed until it's saved; after that, hide it instead.
  6. Fix anything under Fix before saving, then Save Changes (or Discard Changes).

For lists with Used on settings, Show options used on filters the list to one equipment type.

How changes reach the phones

The website and the app share one set of records on the server. A change you save here reaches each phone at its next sync, which runs every 5 minutes while the app is open, when it's opened, a few seconds after the technician saves something, and from Sync Now in the app's Settings.

You change on the websiteWhat phones do
A customer, machine, technology, or subscriptionShow it after their next sync
A machine option or its settingsOffer the new choices after their next sync
A published formUse it for new inspections; draft inspections move to it when they're next opened
A retired Custom checklistStop offering it for new inspections; inspections already using it keep it
A store's contactsUpdate the customers' Who to contact
A person's access turned offThey can't sign in or sync until it's back on, and the app removes their records from that phone

It works the other way too. Customers and machines added or changed in the app appear here after that phone syncs, and each machine shows how many finalized inspections it has. The inspections themselves, with their photos and reports, are read in the app. A phone without a signal keeps everything safe and syncs when it's back online. A machine a customer changes in their app shows Changed by the customer here and in the app until someone checks it.

Promotions

Offers for customers whose machines fit them: a renewal discount for subscriptions expiring soon, an add-on for planters without section control, a check-up for machines not inspected in a year. Customers see them in the app on Home and on the machine, and tap I'm Interested (a lead for you) or Not Now. Owners, and staff an owner gave Can manage promotions (People & stores), write offers; everyone else on staff can look.

To write one:

  1. Tap New Offer. Enter a title, the deal (such as "10% off a year"), a description, an optional code, and when it starts and ends.
  2. Choose the stores (every store, or some), then add conditions under Who sees it: a customer (one or more, found by name), equipment type, make, model year, guidance system, steering, correction source, receiver accuracy, a component (such as a display whose model contains "750"), a feature installed or not, a subscription expiring within so many days, expired, or none, and inspections (the last one had a failed check or a recommendation, or none in so many months). A machine has to meet every condition; with none, every customer machine fits. For an offer just for one customer, add Customer, type part of their name, and choose them; add another condition to narrow it to some of their machines.
  3. Watch Matches … customers, … machines as you go, and tap See who for the list.
  4. Save Draft to finish later, or Publish. A published offer can't be changed: Stop Offer ends it at once, and Make a Copy starts a new draft from it.

Each offer shows how many customers saw it, were interested, said not now, and bought.

Notifications: customers who turned on notifications about new offers in the app hear about an offer when it starts: when you publish it, if it starts that day, or that morning if it starts later.

Emailing an offer: open a running offer. Email it shows how many farms it fits are ready for the email, how many already had it, how many have no email on their customer record, and how many said no. Tap Email N Farms to send it to the email on each farm's customer record, once per farm; farms whose app logins turned offers off aren't emailed. Up to 200 go a day; any others wait, so tap it again the next day. An offer that starts later can be emailed once it starts. Each email names your organization, shows your mailing address, and has a Stop offer emails link; replies go to the farm's store Sales contact. Owners add the Mailing address at the bottom of the Promotions page; no offer email goes until it's there (US law asks marketing email for one). When a farm stops offer emails, the Activity page says so.

Leads: the Leads tab lists every customer who tapped I'm Interested, with the offer, machine, store, and how to reach them. The store's Sales contact (under Contacts for customers; if there's none, its PTx specialist, then the owners) also gets an email at once. Call or reply, then set Contacted, Sold, or Not interested, and add a note. Only owners (every store's) and Customer lead managers (their own store's) see the Leads tab and change leads; give someone at each store the box on People & stores. Writing offers (Can manage promotions) is a separate box and brings no leads. Staff with neither box don't see Promotions at all.

Notifications

The app can notify staff phones when someone on staff finishes an inspection, when a customer finishes one, and when a customer taps I'm Interested. Each person chooses what they get in the app, under Settings › Notifications; there's nothing to set here. Customer logins can turn on notifications about new offers (see Promotions).

Until they change it, admins get both inspection notifications for their store, customer lead managers get interested customers at their store, owners get interested customers from every store (and can choose stores), and employees get none (they can turn on the inspection ones for their store). Only owners and customer lead managers can get interested-customer notifications. Nobody is notified about their own action, and a finished Custom checklist reaches only admins and owners. The lead email to the store's Sales contact still goes out too.

Activity

Owners see an Activity page: who did what in your organization, newest first. It lists people added, changed, turned off, or removed; logins created and deleted; sign-in emails sent (and any that couldn't be); two-step resets; stores; store contacts; forms published and retired; option changes; offers emailed, farms that stopped offer emails, and the mailing address; GNSS receivers moved between a customer's machines; and customers, machines, and inspections deleted, whether on the website or in the app. Use the buttons along the top to show one kind, and Show More for older entries. Nobody can change or remove an entry. Changes Prime Theory Labs makes for your organization are marked (Prime Theory Labs).

Customers on the website

Customer logins can sign in here too, with the email and password they use in the app. They see only Dashboard, My equipment, and Account, and open on their Dashboard: tiles for their machines, those due for inspection, those not ready, and subscriptions to renew; Your machines, each with when it was last inspected, its latest result, and a three-year timeline (each dot is an inspection: click one to open it); Inspections, every finished inspection on their machines with what it found and Download Report (the newest one is already open); and the Subscription tracker, every subscription on their machines by renewal date. My equipment lists their farm's machines with Add Machine; a machine opens the same page you use, without the customer and store choices (theirs are fixed). They can change details, answers, technology, components, features, and subscriptions, archive a machine (only you can bring it back), and delete one with no inspections. Everything they add or change shows Changed by the customer to you, as it does from the app.

Account

Your Account page shows who you're signed in as and turns two-step sign-in on or off. With it on, Field Ready asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone after your password, on the website and in the app, so someone who learns your password still can't get in. Owners should turn it on.

  1. Tap Set Up Two-Step Sign-In.
  2. In your authenticator app (such as Apple Passwords, Google Authenticator, or Microsoft Authenticator), add an account and scan the QR code, or type the key shown.
  3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows, then tap Turn On.

Turn Off removes it. If someone loses their phone, an owner opens them on People & stores, taps Edit, then Reset Two-Step; they can then sign in with their password and set it up again. Only do this when you're sure it's really them asking.

Setting up a new organization

This part is for Prime Theory Labs. Platform admins see an Organizations page that dealers' staff don't; it works only with two-step sign-in on (see Account). Reset Two-Step next to an owner removes an authenticator they lost. It lists every organization using Field Ready with its stores, how many staff and customer logins it has, and its owners. It shows names and counts only; you can't open or change another organization's records from it.

To add a dealer:

  1. Tap New Organization.
  2. Enter the dealer's name, its first store, and the owner's name and email. The email can't already have a login with another organization: each login belongs to one.
  3. Tap Create Organization. It makes the organization, the store, and the owner's login, and loads the default option lists, the Guidance forms, and the Precision Planting and starter Machine forms. Every form starts as Not reviewed until the dealer marks it reviewed.
  4. Field Ready emails the owner a link to choose their password. If the email couldn't be sent, the card shows a message with the link: tap Copy Message (or Copy Link), send it to them, then tap Done.

The owner chooses their password, signs in, and sets up the rest on the pages above: stores, people, customers, options, and forms. Until they choose a password, their line says Hasn't chosen a password yet. If their link expires, New Password Link next to their name emails another.
