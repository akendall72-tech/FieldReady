Field Ready

Help and Support

Field Ready is an app for farm equipment dealers, their staff, and their customers, for inspecting equipment and its precision technology. It's made by Prime Theory Labs.

Contact us

  • Your account, equipment, or inspections: call your dealer's store. Customers find their store's contacts on the Home screen of the app.
  • A problem with the app: email Prime Theory Labs at andrew@primetheorylabs.com.

Signing in

  • Your dealer creates every account. If you need one, ask your store.
  • Your first sign-in uses a temporary password from your dealer; the app then asks you to choose your own.
  • Forgot your password? Ask your store for a new temporary one.

Your records

  • Everything you do is saved on your device first, so the app works without a signal. It syncs with your dealer when you're back online; Settings shows when it last did.
  • Customers see their own equipment, subscriptions, and finished inspection reports, and can add and update their machines.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings › Delete Account. Your dealer then deletes your login. Inspection and equipment records stay with the dealer as service records. See the privacy policy for details.
